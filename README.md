# 🎮 Alice in Borderland

![Alice in Borderland Poster](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcQp-JpQE0IMqc_Tn3O-1aVXvnMtJhqNQwKidw&s)

## Overview
**Alice in Borderland** is a Japanese science fiction thriller series based on the manga by **Haro Aso**.  
It was released on **Netflix** in **2020** and follows a group of people trapped in a deserted version of Tokyo where they must play deadly games to survive.

---

## Setting
The story takes place in an alternate version of **Tokyo**, known as the *Borderland*.  
The city is empty, and mysterious playing cards determine the difficulty and type of each survival game.

---

## Main Characters
- **Arisu (Kento Yamazaki)** – A gamer who becomes the group’s strategist  
- **Usagi (Tao Tsuchiya)** – A mountain climber who teams up with Arisu  
- **Chishiya (Nijiro Murakami)** – A cunning and intelligent player  
- **Kuina (Aya Asahina)** – A brave fighter with a mysterious past  

> “In this world, you have to fight just to stay alive.”
